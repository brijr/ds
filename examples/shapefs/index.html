Questions

Does my agent need anything installed?

No. Shape is a remote MCP server. Add it to Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor or any app that takes MCP connectors, then sign in in your browser. Agents without a browser sign in with a code.

Claude can already read my Google Drive. Why Shape?

Keep using Drive when it does the job. Shape gives your team and approved agents one shared file workspace, with image previews, saved versions and permission controls for each connection. Claude's documentation lists PNG and JPEG support in Drive, while its document picker exports Docs as text without embedded images. Check the route and file type you use; Shape's Drive comparison explains the distinction.

Do I have to move everything in by hand?

No. Drag whole folders into Shape in your browser, or let an agent do the migration: Claude Code, Codex or Cursor can take a folder on your computer, synced Google Drive and Dropbox folders included, rebuild it in Shape and upload every file.

Is it safe to let an agent into our files?

Each agent gets only what you approve, and you can revoke it at any time. It can't delete anything permanently, every change keeps the version before it, and Activity shows every change it made. Shape doesn't have SOC 2 yet.

Do agents cost extra?

No. There are no seats, for people or agents. Every plan includes a monthly allowance of agent requests, and on Pro and Business your agents keep working past it.

Which files does Shape preview?

Thumbnails for JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC, SVG and video. Video and audio play in the browser, and PDFs, fonts, Markdown and text open in place. Everything else is stored and downloads exactly as it went in.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing disappears overnight. After a plan ends there are 7 days of grace, then 30 days read-only so you can download everything. Your agents can still read it too.
